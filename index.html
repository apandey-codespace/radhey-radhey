App Information

Developed With Love

This app is developed with devotion to Radha Krishna. May their blessings be with you always.

राधे राधे

How to Use

  • • Tap the center button to increment count by 1
  • • Long press for continuous counting
  • • Every 108 counts completes one mala
  • • Use reset button to go back to last checkpoint
  • • Your progress is saved automatically
  • • Set your personal mantra by tapping the mantra container

Features

  • • Beautiful minimalist design
  • • Daily and lifetime counters
  • • Mala completion celebrations
  • • Progress statistics
  • • Custom mantra setting
  • • Works offline
  • • No ads, completely free

Spiritual Significance

Chanting Radha's name brings peace, happiness and spiritual progress. Regular japa purifies the heart and connects us with divine love.
